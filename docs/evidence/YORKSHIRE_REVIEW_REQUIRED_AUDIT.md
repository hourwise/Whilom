## Temporal false-positive audit

The known defect class was a century fragment extracted inside a longer token. The current canonical extractor requires a word boundary after the one- or two-digit C-century token, so **C1827**, **C1854**, **C1800**, **C1901** and longer C-prefixed numeric identifiers do not produce century claims. NHLE **1250727** is absent from the temporal evidence rows in this checkpoint.

The corpus audit found 1 C-prefixed longer-token records and 0 claims from that class; the known record is therefore suppressed. It found 7715 records with numeric-looking name material and 89 numeric-name records with claims under the current explicit grammar. Bare address/grid/range/measurement numbers remain unclassified. The current review queue itself contains 6 numeric-looking names; these do not become publication-safe merely because their match decision is separately reviewable. Registered battlefield four-digit claims are limited to the 7 event-context records reported in the JSON evidence.

## Deterministic remediation decision

No additional production classifier change is made in DATA-R1. The current review queue is an identity/governance queue, not a safe temporal-claim queue. Its ambiguous identity, designation-area, conflict, distance and naming cases are correctly fail-closed. The existing lexical C-century boundary correction is general and already present at this checkpoint; this audit adds regression evidence without rewriting canonical generated files.

Recommended future rules remain: explicit lexical boundaries for abbreviated centuries, explicit date grammar for four-digit years, and default-to-no-claim for ambiguous numeric evidence.
